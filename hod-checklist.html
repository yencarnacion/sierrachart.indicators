1) Catalyst / News

Strong catalyst (earnings, FDA, contract, sector momentum) vs no-news random grind.

2) Relative Volume (RVOL)

RVOL ≥ 3 supports momentum; ≤ 1.5 tends to chop/revert.

3) Market Context

Indices (SPY/QQQ) aligned strengthen breakouts; divergence favors fades.

4) Higher Timeframe Confluence

Daily/weekly trend & levels: clean continuation vs extended into resistance.

5) Tape & Order Flow

Bid absorption & reload on retest vs bids pulling/ask stacking.

6) Time of Day

Open & power hour are cleaner; midday tends to whipsaw.

7) VWAP & Anchors

Holding/reclaiming VWAP or anchored VWAP from open/catalyst vs rejection.

8) Liquidity / Spread

Tighter is cleaner (higher win rate); thin names wick & spoof.

9) Volatility / ATR

Early ATR expansion = room to run; 3–5× daily ATR = reversion risk.

10) Confirmation Retest

Defended retest & higher low vs failed retest from below (rejection).
